One letter, one choice: **B — for tea**.

Coffee shouts. Tea waits. Coffee is the deadline, the third alarm, the
meeting that could have been an email. Tea is the pause between the two
halves of a long day, the cup that is still warm when you come back to it,
the answer that does not need to prove anything. The competition asks for a
single letter, A for coffee or B for tea, and the honest answer is B: the
drink that lets the day slow down enough to enjoy it.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt   # B
```
